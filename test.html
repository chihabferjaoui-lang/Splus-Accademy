Test Your English Level

Take a level-specific sample or let a mixed test estimate your current level.

Test Details:
  • Each test has 25 questions selected from a 7,500-question bank at each level
  • Current students can choose their level and compare their latest score
  • New students get a random placement test with questions not previously used in this browser
  • Grammar, vocabulary, reading, and functional language
  • Review and change answers before submitting

I'm already a student — check my progress

Choose your current level. You'll get 25 random questions from that level and see how your score compares with your previous attempt on this browser.

I'm new — find my level

Take a random 25-question test spanning A1–C2. Questions you've already seen in this placement test on this browser won't be used again until a balanced test can no longer be assembled.

Question-use history and progress scores are saved only in this browser. They do not sync across devices or browsers.

Test Format

  • 25 questions per sample
  • Multiple choice format
  • Grammar, vocabulary, reading, and functional language
  • 7,500 questions available at each CEFR level

Proficiency Levels

  • A1 · Beginner
  • A2 · Elementary
  • B1 · Intermediate
  • B2 · Upper Intermediate
  • C1 · Advanced
  • C2 · Proficient

Tips for Success

  • Take your time to read each question carefully
  • Select your best answer before moving to the next question
  • You can move between questions and change answers before finishing
  • Your results are instant and confidential
  • Current students can retake level tests anytime to track their progress
  • Placement questions rotate until a balanced test can no longer be assembled from unused questions

Important: This multiple-choice test estimates reading and language knowledge. It does not assess speaking, listening, or extended writing, and is not a certified CEFR exam.
